title: My usage of AI
date: November 12, 2026
---

First of all, as you could probably tell, there is no AI being used to modify the contents of these journal entries, and nothing being used to correct my grammar or make my stuff easier to read besides myself. <br>
Which, is a great thing in order to express my genuine self on my website! But probably a bad thing for you to be able to read my journals easily. Of course, my mind is pobably a little bit messier than a robot.<br>
And that statement right there, sums up pretty well how I feel about using AI for things. Like yeah, AI can be a pretty cool tool (and to clarify, by AI im mostly referring to commercial large language models that most people use nowadays) But like most tools, it needs to be leveraged in the correct way in order to be effective. <br>
This website is a great example. I've typed out every letter of the code that makes up this website. And I deliberately chose to do this. I could have done what people refer to as 'vibe coding', basically just telling the ai to make changes and only writing out the prompts, while having no clue what the code is doing underneath. And yeah, im gonna be straight forward and admit that would have been a lot easier! <br>
Instead, I chose to take the harder route. I did talk to a language model to help me make this website, however I was careful to only prompt it when i need quick answers to things such as specific documentation, how people go about animating or coding certain things, or "help me i forgot how im supposed to center this text for the 50th time." Things in which 5 years ago I would get the same answer from spending an extra 2 minutes doing a google search and searching through forums. Nowadays it's just faster asking a language model.<br>
Taking this route was harder, but it was a lot more fun and It made me learn so much more! I feel like, if I was less strict in my use of AI and just copy pasted a ton of stuff for this website, I'd feel, i don't know. First of all I'd feel less accomplished, because I wouldn't learn as much. But I'd also feel this kind of like, disconnection. I'd feel more disconnected from my website, because I'd feel less like *I* built it. This website here is my baby. Okay maybe it's not that serious. But it's my creation! It's my art, yknow? And delegating too many tasks to a robot can remove some of the passion that makes up the beauty of art.
