the journal entries that you'll read here are a result of me deciding to sit down with myself and write about whatever's on my mind for a lil bit.<br>
you're mostly gonna find some psychology/philosophy related stuff, small rants, small life stories, some advice that I like to give to other people, some life lessons that I've learned, or me nerding out about something I made or am interested in.<br>
oh ya as a warning, some of the topics could be sensitive to some people.<br>

As a result, I might end up misspeling something, what I state to be fact could probably be wrong, and my wording/sentences might be a bit difficult to understand.<br>
However, that also means that what I write out below will be as genuine as possible of what my mind produces. <br>
be sure to draw your own conclusions and think for yourself. <br>
