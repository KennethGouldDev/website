---
title: My usage of AI
date: November 12, 2026
---

First of all, as you could probably tell, there is no AI being used to modify the contents of these journal entries, and nothing being used to correct my grammar or make my stuff easier to read besides myself.<br>
Which, is a great thing in order to express my genuine self on my website! But probably a bad thing for you to be able to read my journals easily. Of course, my mind and my writing skills are pobably going to be a little bit messier than a robot.<br>
And that intro right there, sums up pretty well how I feel about using AI for things. Like yeah, AI can be a pretty cool tool (and to clarify, by AI im mostly referring to commercial large language models that most people use nowadays) But like most tools, it needs to be leveraged in the correct way in order to be effective. <br>
This website is a great example. I've typed out every letter of the code that makes up this website, and I deliberately chose to do so. I could have done what most people who don't really care for learning or programming do, basically just telling an ai to make changes and only writing out prompts, while having no clue what the code is doing underneath. And yes, I want to be straight forward and admit, that that would have been a lot easier! I would have gotten results quicker, and in this day and age even a bit *better* than what I ended up with.<br>
However, instead I chose to take the harder route. I did talk to a language model to help me make this website, however I was very careful to only prompt it when i need quick answers to things such as specific documentation, how people go about animating or coding certain things, or "help me i forgot how im supposed to center this text for the 50th time." Things, in which 5 years ago, I would end up with the same solution but just from having to spend an extra 2 minutes doing a google search and searching through forums. Nowadays it's just faster asking a language model.<br>
Taking this route was harder, but it was a lot more fun and It made me learn so much more! I feel like, if I was less strict in my use of AI, and i just copy pasted a ton of stuff for this website, I'd feel, i don't know. <br>
First of all I'd feel less accomplished, because I wouldn't learn as much. But I'd also feel this kind of like, disconnection. I'd feel more disconnected from my website, because I'd feel less like **I** built it. This website here is my baby. Maybe it's not *that* serious, but it *is* **my creation**! It's my art, yknow?<br>
And in my opinion, when you delegate too many tasks to something like a robot, that removes a lot of the passion that is involved with creating art. Part of what makes art beautiful is the struggle, the imperfections, and most importantly the expression of a human being. <br>
Remember next time you create something, *anything*, that part of what makes that creation valuable is that *you* made it. Don't stifle that inherent value that you have, yknow? Take advantage of it, learn from it, appreciate it for what it is.
