date: November 27, 2026

this is a pretty raw journal, and more of a rant about my current circumstances and the thoughts that I've been having because of it. 

this is such a weird time because this is technically one of the hardest parts of my life I've ever been through right now. Working through a lot of emotion and pain and memories that my brain is clinging onto, is very hard. Life is changing very drastically for me, however I don't necessarily *only* feel like im being swept up in the wind with it, I feel kinda the opposite too.
I wake up in my bed. And sometimes it takes me like an hour or two to work up the courage to get up and out. My body has been feeling very tired, like a deep tired in all of my limbs. Sometimes my brain either feels like its being drilled by a power tool from the hand of the inner voice in my mind, or it feels like im just a zombie barely able to keep my eyes fully open. 
Its like, I don't have much motivation, but at the same time I do, because if you were to watch me from the outside, Yes, you would see me kind of wasting away every now and then for a bit. But then you would also see me get up and still do absolutely everything that I need to get done.
My room is always neat. My bathroom is always wiped down. My laundry never overflows. My trash can is always taken out. First thing I do when I get out of bed, is make my bed, brush my teeth as if my dentist was watching me, and put on my running shoes ready to kill a workout. I get home exhausted, shower, and then maybe cry or scream a bit in bed as I fight to get my meditation done and ten pages of reading done. I cook a healthy meal and resist as much as i can of any temptations to overeat or eat something tastier than a slab of meat. One of the things I look forward to the most in my day is the taste of my sweet succulent protein shake, or the feeling of the soft freshly washed blanket and sheets in my bed as I hug my pillow at night.
A lot of this stuff, now that i'm writing it out, seems pretty basic. Like, anybody who has their life together can do all this healthy shit, because it's probably already a baseline for them. However, like literally just a month or two ago, I was not doing any of this healthy stuff. I was very depressed, I was barely showering, I couldn't even really look someone in the eyes for too long without feeling a lingering sense of shame. 
The thing is, I still kind of have this kind of mind, of one who has a lot of emotion kept inside, of one who still has these old habits of not taking care of themselves properly. And so, if I still have this mind, why am I doing all of this healthy shit? Anyone who is depressed usually doesn't want to, or let alone *actually does* the things that I'm doing right now. It feels so hard. So hard to get up in the morning and knowing I'm going to do all this hard shit and probably have a couple breakdowns in the day ahead of me. 
But as I lay looking up at the ceiling, I look at the dichotomy in front of me: to either lay in bed all day sulking, or get up and do this bullshit that my past self wanted me to do. I look at that first option of just taking a break and doing nothing for a day, and boy oh boy does it seem pretty appealing. 
but then I look at the second option, and I think, "well, if I *don't* do all this, then what the fuck *else* am I gonna do??" Like seriously though.
You see, my past self figured it would probably be healthy of me to work toward my goals of having a better physique and a better mind. My past self put up this habit tracker on my wall, and put subtle reminders around of what I want to work toward and what i want to have in life, hoping that I would just buckle down and build something better. 
And now here I am. At rock bottom. With nothing to do. And that's exactly the problem!! Nothing to fucking do!! The only thing that I HAVE that I can do is this rough plan that my past self made for me. And remember if I *don't* do whatever my past self wanted, *what else am i gonna do*?? I'd end up doing nothing! because theres nothing else to do!! 
If I don't do this plan then ill just end up as that same person a few months ago again, that wanted to do nothing but stew in my room!! Why would I do nothing right now, just to end up having nothing again in the future. If I have nothing, then I might as well try to do SOMETHING to change that. 
And with that thinking, I just push through it anyway. And from pushing through, i've also figured out, that all this shit, the journaling, the meditation, the pages, the weights, even working on this fucking website, I'm not just doing all this because I *want* to. I'm doing all this because I **NEED** to.
Between all the fucking breakdowns that I have everyday, I feel numb. Between all the sounds of loud music i like blasting in my ears, I hear silence -- but a specific kind of silence, a silence that feels like everything around you is surrounded by fog, and you're alone with just your brain saying things to you.
When I'm not doing anything, that's what it feels like. But when I **AM** doing this stuff, taking on these simple but challenging tasks, it actually makes me FEEL SOMETHING!! So, if I don't go to the gym, im not going to be feeling anymore!! It feels like im gonna die if I dont do this shit because just what the fuck is even the point if I don't have these anchors ground me. It's so weird, it's like I almost don't even feel like a person if i dont perform these arbitrary constraints that I've imposed on myself. 
And you want to know the worst part?? this stuff is WORKING. It's fucking working! this plan that that asshole, the past me, made for myself, is actually working! Between the numbness i feel, and my muscles giving out on the chest press machine, my mind is getting better! I'm having healthier thoughts! The negative thoughts that my past self would deal with are slowly going away! When I talk to people, I feel more concious and appreciative!
I even love all the breakdowns. I mentioned before that it kind of felt like my emotions were kept inside, like they were clogged. It felt like I couldn't express myself properly or get everything out. Now I have the opportunity to let everything out, to express myself freely, figure out what I'm experiencing, and be more genuine and honest with the world and myself than ever. Overall, every day is getting a little bit better, it's getting easier and easier to get out of bed, and I feel like even more of an individual.
Whew, alright, atleast that was an optimistic note to end on. Again this is just an emotional state I'm in rn. very interesting.
